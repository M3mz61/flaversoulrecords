Lizzie France

Elizabeth France is a London Based Vocalist with many releases within the underground house music scene, working with producer Memz most recently with her soulful vocals on the disco inspired 'Living Without Your Love' (Radio Edition & Club Edition Remix) reaching 17 in the Beatport Hype Dance/Pop Genre despite mis-categorisation her solid performance on that track must have an effect on the listeners.
