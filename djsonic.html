DJ Sonic

With roots deep in the mid-90s UK underground scene, DJ Sonic has spent decades driving the airwaves and dancefloors. As the operator of Evolution Radio UK, his crate-digging and genre fluidity span the definitive lineage of British electronic music heritage—from Acid House, Hardcore, and Jungle, to UKG and Drum & Bass. No trends, no corporate filler, just pure underground rotation.
